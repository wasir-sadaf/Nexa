# Nexa Knowledge Base


## Refund Policy


Customers can request a refund within 30 days of receiving an order.


Refund requests must be for an eligible purchase.


Refund requests are reviewed before approval.


The refund amount and processing time may depend on the payment method and the result of the review.


## Return Policy


Customers can return eligible products within 30 days of delivery.


Returned products should be unused and in acceptable condition.


Some products may not be eligible for return.


## Shipping Policy


Standard shipping normally takes 3-5 business days.


Delivery time may vary depending on the customer's location and other circumstances.


Customers can contact support if their shipment is significantly delayed.


## Payment


Nexa supports standard online payment methods.


If a payment fails, customers should try the payment again or contact customer support.


If a customer was charged but the order was not created, they should contact customer support.


## Customer Support


Customers can contact Nexa support when they have an issue that cannot be resolved through the available information.


Customers can also request to speak with a human support agent.


## General Information


Nexa is an AI-supported customer service system.


The system can help customers with order information, support tickets, refunds, returns, shipping questions, and human support escalation.
